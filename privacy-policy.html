Sky Weather — Privacy Policy Effective date: 26 September 2026 App: com.ankilla.skyweather Contact: supportapp247@gmail.com Sky Weather is provided by an individual developer. It is free, with no advertising, purchases, user accounts, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated server. Location and purpose With your permission, the app obtains your device location while you use it. Approximate or precise coordinates, depending on your Android permission choice, are sent over HTTPS to Open-Meteo for local weather and air quality. On Android, coordinates are also passed to the system Geocoder to obtain an area name. That service may communicate with its network provider under the provider's privacy terms. The app does not request background location or maintain a location history. Automatic loading When you reopen the app, it checks your existing Android permission and loads local weather. It may refresh when you return after ten minutes. You can also refresh manually. You can revoke permission or change location accuracy in Android Settings at any time. Without location permission, this version cannot load local weather. Providers and retention Open-Meteo receives the requested coordinates and network information such as your IP address. Its free-API policy states that troubleshooting logs may contain coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. These provider logs are not controlled by the app developer. See https://open-meteo.com/en/terms and contact info@open-meteo.com for provider privacy requests. Android's system geocoding provider manages any service data under its own terms; its availability and provider can vary by device. The app keeps fetched weather and the area name in working memory. It does not write a location database or account profile to device storage or a developer server. Android itself retains your permission choice. The app does not sell your information or use it for advertising. Support If you email supportapp247@gmail.com, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained while handling your request and until you request deletion, unless retention is legally required. Email is handled by the email service provider. Your choices and deletion Revoke location permission in Android Settings to stop further location requests from the app, or uninstall it. There is no account to delete. Contact supportapp247@gmail.com about support correspondence or privacy questions. For data held by a weather or system service provider, contact that provider directly; uninstalling the app does not erase its server logs. Security Weather and air-quality requests use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed completely secure. Android manages system location permissions and its geocoding service. Children The app is a general weather tool and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly solicit children's personal details or create child profiles. Changes Updates to this policy will be made available in the app and on its published privacy-policy page, with an updated effective date.
